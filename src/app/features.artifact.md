# Detailed Features Content

## 1. Attendance Tracking
**Overview**: Real-time monitoring of student presence using mobile and web interfaces.
**Details**:
- **Automated Alerts**: Instantly notify parents via SMS or App notifications when a student is marked absent or arrives late.
- **Biometric Integration**: Support for digital attendance logs that can be synced with biometric devices if available.
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify patterns of chronic absenteeism early to intervene before academic performance is affected.
- **Teacher Accountability**: Log exactly when and by whom attendance was taken for every lesson.

## 2. Smart Analytics
**Overview**: Data-driven insights to improve student outcomes and teacher efficiency.
**Details**:
- **Predictive Risk Modeling**: Our AI identifies students at risk of dropping out or failing based on historical trends and current performance.
- **Value-Add Tracking**: Measure teacher impact by comparing student performance at the start of the term vs the end, accounting for entry behavior.
- **CBC Competency Mapping**: Visual maps of student strengths across various competencies, helping teachers focus on specific learning gaps.
- **Comparative Benchmarking**: Compare your school's performance against regional averages or previous years' data.

## 3. Parent Portal
**Overview**: A transparent window into the student's academic life for guardians.
**Details**:
- **Real-Time Results**: Parents can view exam results and report cards as soon as they are published by the headteacher.
- **Fee Transparency**: View current balances, payment history, and download official receipts directly from the portal.
- **Discipline Logs**: Stay informed about disciplinary actions or positive behavior commendations.
- **Teacher Communication**: Secure channel for parents to receive updates from class teachers regarding their child's progress.

## 4. Fee Management
**Overview**: Streamlined financial operations for school bursars and administrators.
**Details**:
- **Automated Invoicing**: Generate and send fee structures to all parents at the start of the term with one click.
- **Payment Reconciliation**: Integrated tracking of bank deposits, M-Pesa payments, and cash, reducing manual bookkeeping errors.
- **Defaulter Management**: Automatically generate lists of students with outstanding balances and send polite reminders to parents.
- **Financial Reporting**: Real-time cash flow statements and income summaries for school management boards.
